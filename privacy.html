Privacy Policy — Acid-Base Scout (beta)

Last updated: 16 August 2026. Acid-Base Scout is a clinical decision-support app in a closed evaluation. This policy describes what the app and this site process.

Photos and laboratory values

  • Photos of the analyzer screen are processed entirely on the device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.
  • The values read from a photo, and any case data you enter, stay in the app's local storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app instructs users to keep patient-identifying information out of the frame and out of all text fields. Do not enter patient identifiers anywhere in the app.

Licensing

  • On first run the app generates a random 8-character Device ID. It identifies the installation, not you, and contains no personal or hardware information.
  • When you request an unlock key on this site, we store the pairing of your invite code with that Device ID, so a code cannot be reused on another phone.

Signup form

  • If you request an invite code through the signup form, we store the name and email you enter, together with the code issued and the event it belongs to, for the duration of the evaluation. They are used only to administer the trial and to notify participants before the beta version is retired. Never shared, never used for marketing.

Feedback

  • Feedback is voluntary. A report includes your text, the app version, technical scan details, and the Device ID — no photos, no patient data, no location.
  • An email address is stored only if you choose to provide one, and is used only to reply or to send a TestFlight invitation.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or advertising trackers, on the site or in the app.
  • No sale or sharing of any data with third parties.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no patient records.

Retention and contact

Licensing pairings and feedback are kept for the duration of the evaluation and deleted after it ends. To have your feedback or email removed earlier, write to alexross48@gmail.com.

Acid-Base Scout is decision support, not a diagnosis, and is not a registered medical device. © 2026 Alex Ross.
