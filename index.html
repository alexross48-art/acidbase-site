Questions

What does it read?

The blood-gas panel straight off the lab screen: pH, pCO₂, pO₂, electrolytes, lactate, haemoglobin fractions and more. Values it can't read with confidence it leaves blank rather than guessing — a blank can be re-shot; a plausible wrong number can't be unseen.

Where do the photos go?

Nowhere. Reading happens on the phone. Photos are not uploaded, and the app asks you to keep the patient banner out of frame.

What does it do with the values?

A structured acid-base reading: primary disorder, anion gap, expected compensation — with the arithmetic shown at every step. It may suggest adding the chemistry or urine panel when they would change the interpretation.

Who can join?

Invited clinicians in the closed trial. The ten most active testers keep the full version free when it launches.

Feedback / bug reports

Anything — a misread value, a wrong interpretation, an idea. Please, no patient details.

Decision support, not a diagnosis. Acid-Base Scout is an evaluation build and not a registered medical device. Check every value read from a photo against the original results. Clinical decisions remain yours.
